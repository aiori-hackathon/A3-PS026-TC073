What this tool does

Parses AT URIs of the form at://authority/collection/rkey, validates each component, and compares the results of four parsing strategies to surface real disagreements.

Parsing strategies

  • standard — AT-Protocol-aware: DID colons stay in the authority; NSID and record-key grammar enforced.
  • strict — additionally enforces method-specific rules (e.g. did:plc identifier is a single segment).
  • compatibility — lenient; accepts inputs the underlying URI libraries would accept, warns on host:port ambiguity.
  • rfc3986 — simulates a generic parser that treats the first colon in the authority as host:port, exposing DID mis-splits.

Corpus

110 cases across 10 categories: valid, invalid, did_colon, handle, path, encoding, unicode, malformed, conflict, boundary. Each case has an expected annotation; ambiguous cases are tagged review rather than being labelled pass/fail.
